マンダラチャート Mandal-Art Goal Chart

Put one goal in the center. Surround it with 8 sub-goals, and give each sub-goal 8 concrete actions. 1 + 8 + 64 = 81 cells.

What is Mandal-Art?

Mandal-Art (マンダラチャート) is a Japanese goal-setting and brainstorming method created by designer Hiroaki Imaizumi and popularized by consultant Yasuo Matsumura. It became widely known after baseball star Shohei Ohtani used it as a high-schooler.

  • Center block: your main goal sits in the very middle, ringed by 8 sub-goals.
  • Outer blocks: each sub-goal moves to the center of one outer block, surrounded by 8 actions that make it happen.
  • Editing a sub-goal updates both its copies automatically.
  • Everything you type is saved in your browser (localStorage).
